Hey @Aman! Thanks a ton for sharing the draft. We've gone through it and genuinely quite like the direction and overall feel of the video — it's coming together really nicely. A few small refinements for the intro, and if you could incorporate these, it would elevate the flow even further:

Refinements
4 items
1
Talking Pace Delivery Adjust

Slightly faster talking pace overall — not rushed at all, just a touch more fluid and conversational. The current pacing feels slightly deliberate; a natural pickup would make it feel more authentic.

2
Opening Frame — Shirt Presentation On-Camera Reshoot

In the very first face-to-camera shot, if we could have the shirt looking a little more neat and freshly ironed, that would help the opening frame feel even cleaner. First impressions matter for premium fashion content.

3
Dialogue Change Script Reshoot

In the next line, could we change the dialogue from the current line to include the product name explicitly?

Current
"So I ordered this shirt"

"So I ordered this 100% Egyptian Cotton Shirt"

Shirt Reveal — Transition Timing Direction Adjust

At the line "their everyday formal collection is absolutely insane", could you transition into wearing the shirt instead of staying face-to-camera? Ideally by the word "insane" the shirt is fully on so that reaction lands with the reveal.
